📊 How to Use

Add Fins: Use the controls on the left to add causes to the top or bottom of the fishbone.
Reorder: Drag and drop fins in the lists to change their timeline sequence.
Remove: Click the delete button on any fin to remove it.
Save/Load: Save your diagram as a JSON file or load a previously saved diagram.
