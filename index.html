📊 How to Use

Add Events: Add events to the top or bottom of the timeline using the controls on the left.
Reorder: Drag and drop events in the combined list to arrange them in your desired timeline sequence.
Remove: Click the delete button on any event to remove it.
Mix & Match: You can have multiple bottom events, then top events, in any order you like!
